Polk Audio: Zen Den vs. Rave Cave

One soundbar and two very different personalities. I split the product read between the hosts as a running bit, so every feature landed twice.

Polk Audio script begins at 1:10. Watch on YouTube · Rewind for full-show context.

Cold open

ChelseaPaul, what the heck are you doing over there?

AJShhhh, Chelsea, I'm in my Zen Den.

ChelseaUm, what?

AJShhhh… My friends over at Polk Audio sent me this incredible soundbar, the Polk Audio Magnifi Max AX. It's ultra-compact, with Dolby Atmos and DTS:X! It also has a 10" wireless subwoofer for an ultra-deep bass experience. I can Chromecast or Apple AirPlay my sweet meditation sounds to it, allowing me to completely shut out the world, and it's like I'm right in the middle of the ocean with the songs of whales to comfort my troubles away.

ChelseaSongs of whales to meditate? I swear this is why people never send you anything—

AJI can't hear you, Chelsea, I'm at PEACE!

ChelseaWell, I actually got a soundbar of my own from MY friends at Polk Audio. And you know what? Instead of a "Zen Den," I'm making a Rave Cave. I set up my Magnifi Max AX with Dolby Atmos and a wireless subwoofer, and I am stoked to have this brand new eleven-speaker, fully immersive sound system going at full blast! And after I'm done doing my cardio in my Rave Cave, I'm going to sit back and enjoy all my games, TV shows and movies with crisp, clear, immersive audio. I can hear every word of dialogue with patented VoiceAdjust technology. Paul, how does that sound?

AJYou know what, Chelsea, I didn't listen to a word you were saying. I was in total Zen.

AJAll Polk Audio soundbars are on sale now. I think you guys know what I'm about to say… Chelsea, you wanna say it with me?

BothSCAN THAT QR CODE!

Giveaway read

ChelseaWith the Polk Audio Signa S4 you get cinema-quality sound at a value you couldn't imagine. This seven-speaker soundbar gives you powerful sound and deep bass with a wireless subwoofer, all with the vocal clarity of Polk's VoiceAdjust technology. Guess the correct answer to trivia this round and you'll have a chance to win!

Progressive: "Scan the code"

Car insurance on a gaming show, with one message to land: save nearly $700. I turned the call to action into a running gag the audience could expect.

Progressive script begins at 1:38. Watch on YouTube · Rewind for full-show context.

Cold open

AJIf you've never seen Fight Night before, welcome to the Season 3 finale. And today, Fight Night is brought to you by Progressive: people who save by switching to Progressive save nearly $700. Just letting you know, Chelsea.

ChelseaYou always direct these things at me, Paul, and if anything I should be telling you this. You are, and continue to be, an absolute liability on this show.

AJWhat? I'm not a liability, I'm the voice of reason, Chelsea! And this voice of reason, chat, is telling you to scan the dang QR code! You wanna save big? Scan the code. You want nearly $700 in savings for new customers? Scan the code. You wanna squad up with Flo? Scan the code. You want Chelsea's mic muted? Scan the code. I bring the pizzazz to this show, you know what I'm saying?

ChelseaI don't, and I don't think production does either.

Toss to break

AJChat, don't go AFK on these savings. We'll be back with more Fight Night after these savings…

Branded segment

ChelseaIt's our finale, and what better way to end a season than with Progressive? Normally we don't get any context for what we're about to watch, but because this is a special episode, I've been told we're going to look at some moments from this show where we could have really used some coverage.

ChelseaLooking back at all the times I needed coverage makes me want to remind y'all that Progressive has incredible coverage, and switching could save you nearly $700 on average.

Final toss to break

AJWhen you see that QR code on screen, I want you to scan like you've never scanned before, like you've been training for this moment your whole life. Don't let me down, chat. I'm counting on you. Scan it.

Outro

ChelseaA big thank you to our partner Progressive for being with us for this season finale. And remember, y'all: people who save by switching to Progressive save nearly $700. That's a lot of dang money.

JBL Quantum: "I'm kinda a big deal"

A headset spec sheet rewritten as one host's bragging, with the other host's comeback delivering the call to action.

JBL script begins at 1:00. Watch on YouTube · Rewind for full-show context.

Cold open

AJNow Chelsea, I'm not sure if you're aware of this, but I'm kinda a big deal.

ChelseaOh, are you now?

AJYes, I am. Not only because of my incredible personality and dashing looks, but also because I'm rocking my new JBL Quantum Ones. Chat, look at how good I look! With my JBL Quantum Ones, I, personally, can change the game. I get hyper-accurate, immersive sound to hear every. single. detail. in the world around me. Remember when we played Overwatch 2 and I was carrying the team like a backpack? And you guys did nothing? NOT ANYMORE. I've got a direct line to hear every ping, every footstep, all with the superior sound of JBL Quantum. You can't stop me now, Chelsea!

ChelseaChat, I don't know why Paul feels the need to take up so much room on this show. If you want the competitive advantage to take on any challenge, like trying to host a show with Paul, you need JBL Quantum. Scan the QR code or hit the link in chat to find the perfect headset for you.

Toss to gameplay

AJYou know what else we deserve, Chelsea? Superior sound from our new JBL Quantum gaming headsets. It's time to become Legends. Let's go!

Final toss to break

AJAs a reminder, chat: you can't change the game unless you have game-changing audio. I've been rocking this JBL Quantum gaming headset all show, and you can too. When you see my beautiful face on screen, SCAN THAT CODE.

Alienware: Better together

Two hosts, two halves of a gaming setup. A list of hardware specs became a sibling rivalry that ended in a giveaway.

Alienware script begins at 1:11. Watch on YouTube · Rewind for full-show context.

Cold open

AJChelsea, I hear you got a few new gifts…

ChelseaWhy yes, these are a few new gifts from my friends at Alienware. They gave me an incredible laptop and monitor last time, and followed it up with a 38-inch Alienware ultrawide curved gaming monitor and a tri-mode wireless gaming mouse. This monitor has lifelike color with VESA DisplayHDR 600 matched with G-SYNC: stunning contrast, cinematic color and ultra-low latency gameplay. And the mouse has 26,000 DPI. Over wireless USB-C or Bluetooth, you get over 140 hours of game time on one charge. Paul, how are you going to top this?

AJRight off the bat, Chelsea, I don't see a computer as part of your set. Not just a computer, a friggin' gaming desktop. For you, Chelsea, I'm going to break this down.

ChelseaYou know what, I see what they did. I think they wanted us to work together, because you can't play on a gaming desktop without a monitor and mouse. We'd have to combine your gear and my gear to get the ultimate Alienware desktop gaming rig. Alienware, I appreciate the gifts, but have you ever tried playing a game with Paul? It is the most frustrating, painful thing I have ever done. You're always screaming, with your sweaty, gross Cheeto fingers and beard hair all over the keybo—

AJANYWAY. We're giving this bad boy away! You don't have to barter a deal with Chelsea. We did it once, and we're doing it again! Stick around till the end of the show for a chance to win by typing !Alienware into the chat.

Toss to gameplay

AJAlright chat, are you ready for a new fantastic point of view? Don't you dare close your eyes… it's time for a whole Neeeew Wooooorld! Let's go!
